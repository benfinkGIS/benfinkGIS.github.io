Who Lives Far from The Loop?

The Chuck Huckelberry Loop is Tucson’s network of shared-use paths, running mostly along the rivers and washes. This project measures how long it takes to bike from home to the nearest point on it, for everyone in the Tucson area, and maps where the people with the longest rides live.

The ride is the shortest legal route over the real street and path network, so a path that passes under a road with no way up to it does not count as an entrance.

      Data sources and methods

      • Ride time: the shortest legal route from home to the nearest point on the Loop, at 10 mph on streets and paved paths, slower on unpaved trails, and walking pace on footways. One-way streets are one-way. Freeways and roads signed no-bikes are left out. Private streets, such as those in gated communities, can only be used by the people who live on them, to get out.
      • What is not counted: traffic. A mile on a fast arterial costs the same as a mile on a quiet street, and most people would not ride every one of these routes. The real reach of the Loop is smaller than shown here, so the count of far-away residents is a low estimate.
      • Streets and paths: OpenStreetMap, from copies of the map dated between July and November 2026. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Two paths only connect where the map data joins them, which is how underpasses and overpasses are kept from counting as entrances.
      • The Loop: an extent of … miles assembled from OpenStreetMap, because no single route there covers all of it, so a few pieces may be missing or extra.
      • Residents: 2020 Census population by block, from the U.S. Census Bureau. Each block’s people are placed at the block’s centre point and joined to the nearest street. That is rough for the large blocks at the desert edge.
      • Communities: 2020 Census cities, towns and census-designated places. “Tucson” means inside the city limits.
      • Study area: …, covering … residents. Another … Pima County residents live outside it, in Green Valley, Sahuarita, Ajo and other places. The closest of them is … miles from the Loop in a straight line. They are not in any figure on this page.
      • Map: hexagon cells … m from centre to corner, about 0.6 square miles each, on Esri’s light gray basemap.
