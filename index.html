Projects _□×

Personal projects.

  • Tucson Golf Course Water Map
    An interactive map exploring water use at golf courses in the Tucson area. Full-page map.
  • Tucson Loop Access
    A network analysis of how long the bike ride is from home to The Loop, who lives far from it, and where it could grow. Full-page maps: who lives far and where it could grow.
  • A Beer Off The Loop
    Every brewery, bar and restaurant licensed to pour within a short ride of The Loop, with the way off the path to each one. Full-page map.
  • Santa Catalinas 3D
    A true-scale 3D model of the Santa Catalina Mountains that you can orbit and zoom, with a sun you can move across the sky. Full-page viewer.
  • Kit Fox Release Sites
    A habitat suitability model for returning a rescued kit fox to the desert, with weights you can change yourself. Full-page map.
  • KXCI Signal Reach
    Who can hear Tucson’s community radio station: the FCC’s predicted service area, set against a terrain model that knows about the mountains. Full-page map.
  • Empty Homes
    How much housing in Pima County stands empty: winter homes owned from out of state, vacant homes and unfinished buildings, set against the count of people without a home. Full-page map.
  • A Northern Station
    Where the proposed Phoenix–Tucson passenger train could stop north of downtown: open land beside the Union Pacific line, screened for room, floodway and a way in, and who could drive to each site. Full-page map.
