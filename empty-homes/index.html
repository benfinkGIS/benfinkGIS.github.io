    Set against the count

    Homes in Pima County, and the people counted without one. The Census rows are estimates for ….

    There are about … winter homes for every person counted. The rows cannot be added together: the winter homes come from the Assessor’s tax roll and the other three from the Census, and a house can be in both.

    Empty is not the same as available. These homes have owners, some need repairs, and a vacancy for rent or for sale is a home between occupants. The count of people is a one-night count and is generally treated as an undercount.

    What counts as a winter home

    The Census cannot pick these out. Its “seasonal” category also holds cabins, timeshares and short-term rentals, and it counts a winter home as occupied when the owners are in it for the season. The Assessor’s tax roll can. A home is counted here when all four of these are true:

    1. It is not a main home and not a rental. The Assessor taxes it as legal class 4.1: nobody has claimed it as a primary residence, and it is not registered as a rental.
    2. The tax bill goes out of state. The owner’s mailing address is outside Arizona. Addresses in Canada and other countries count too.
    3. A person owns it. The owner is a person or a family trust, not a company or a bank.
    4. It is not on Mt Lemmon. Cabins in ZIP code 85619 are left out.

    Vacant for years?

    No public record says which homes have stood empty, or for how long. Three records come close:

    • Vacant-building cases. The City of Tucson has opened … code enforcement cases about vacant or neglected buildings since …. … properties have a case still open, and … of those have been open two years or more. These are buildings someone complained about, inside the city only.
    • Unfinished buildings. The Assessor lists … parcels across the county with housing that is partially complete, … of them apartment sites, and … more with unfinished shops, offices and warehouses. The list includes sites where work is under way.
    • The Census. About … homes in the county are vacant and not for rent, for sale or seasonal: foreclosures, homes in probate, homes needing repair and homes simply held empty. The Census does not ask how long.

    The Postal Service does record how long an address has gone without mail, and HUD publishes those counts by Census tract, including addresses vacant for more than two and more than three years. HUD releases them only to governments and non-profit organisations, so they are not on this page.

    Data sources and methods

    • People without a home: the … Point-in-Time Count by the Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness: … people in shelters and on the street on …, against … the year before.
    • Homes: the Pima County parcel layer published by the City of Tucson, residential parcels with a single-family house, a condominium or townhome, or a manufactured home: … in all. Apartments and duplexes are left out.
    • Legal class: the Pima County Assessor’s Class Master files. Class 4.1 also covers homes that are vacant, for sale or owned by a bank, so a few of the winter homes will be those. … more homes are in class 4.1 with an Arizona mailing address; those are not counted, though some will be second homes or stand empty.
    • What the definition misses: winter residents who have their tax bill sent to the Tucson address, and those who claim the Arizona home as their main one. What it wrongly takes in: homes rented out by an out-of-state owner who has not registered the rental.
    • Hexagons: … metres from centre to corner. A hexagon with fewer than … winter homes is not drawn; … winter homes are counted in the totals and not shown on the map.
    • Census vacancy: American Community Survey …, tables B25002 and B25004. These are survey estimates. For a single tract the margin of error is often as large as the estimate, so read the tract map for the pattern and not for any one tract.
    • Unfinished buildings: parcels the Assessor gives a “partially complete” use code. The map shows the housing ones.
    • Vacant-building cases: City of Tucson code enforcement cases of the types Vacant/Nuisance Buildings, VANS Program and Lien Foreclosure Program. One triangle per property, dated from its oldest open case.
    • Basemap: Esri. Script: tools/build_empty_homes.py in the site’s repository. Data built ….
