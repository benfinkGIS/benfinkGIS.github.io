Data sources and methods

  • Course boundaries: OpenStreetMap (leisure=golf_course), pulled 9 November 2026 and saved with the site, so the map does not depend on a live query. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
  • Imagery and labels: Esri World Imagery, with Esri road and place-name layers on top (switch them off with the Labels button).
  • Study area: courses within about 32 miles of downtown Tucson, which takes in Oro Valley, Marana, Vail, Green Valley, Sahuarita, and the SaddleBrooke communities. Tubac and San Manuel fall outside it.
  • What counts as active: OpenStreetMap does not flag closed courses in this area, so closures were checked against news reports. Removed as closed: Del Lago Golf Club in Vail (closed August 2026), Quail Canyon Golf Course (closed, rezoned for housing), and the Blanchard course at Davis-Monthan Air Force Base (closed 2018). Other courses are assumed open if nothing turned up saying otherwise, so a recent closure could still be on the map.
  • Known gaps: the SaddleBrooke Ranch boundary in OpenStreetMap covers only part of the course (19 acres for 18 holes), so it is flagged. I checked that one against the imagery; the other 40 outlines were not checked one by one. Where a resort has several courses, each is drawn separately.
